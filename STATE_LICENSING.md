# ImpactQuadrant — Business-sale success-fee licensing (US + global)

**NOT LEGAL ADVICE.** Snapshot 2026-10-24 from secondary statute-citing tables and some primary sources. Sources conflict for several states; confirm with counsel before taking any contingent fee.

## Federal M&A broker exemption
Exchange Act §15(b)(13), added by Consolidated Appropriations Act 2023 (Div. AA, Title V), effective **Mar 29, 2023** (IBBA: https://www.ibba.org/resource-center/legal-updates/). Exempts an M&A broker from SEC broker-dealer registration when facilitating transfer of control of an eligible privately held company (no SEC-registered securities; prior-FY EBITDA <$25M or gross revenue <$250M) to a buyer who will control and actively operate it. Not available if broker holds funds/securities, provides financing, deals with shell companies or passive buyers, binds a party, or dual-represents without written consent. Earlier SEC M&A Brokers no-action letter (Jan 2014, not Dec 2022) preceded it. States separately regulate; NASAA amended model rule May 6, 2024 to match (https://www.nasaa.org/71912/nasaa-members-approve-amendments-for-two-model-rules/). ~26 states have M&A relief as of June 2026 (M&A relief column). Asset sales are generally not securities.

## Flat fees
Non-contingent info/listing/verification fees: generally not 'compensation for negotiating a sale' in no-rule states. BUT Nevada NRS 645.030(1)(b) treats **advance fees** for promoting sale of business opportunities as RE brokerage, and RE-licence states may treat listing fees for a business as brokerage. Info products sold to buyers (data, teardowns) are lowest risk.

## Recommended ImpactQuadrant rule
1. Contingent commission only when seller AND buyer are in GREEN states, asset sale, no real estate or lease assignment in the fee basis, no handling of funds.
2. Stock sales: only within federal §15(b)(13) conditions AND state M&A relief (or registered BD partner).
3. Flat-fee info/verification/subscription products everywhere (avoid 'advance listing fees' to sellers in NV and RED states).
4. Geo-gate by seller state, buyer state, and property location; RED/YELLOW → refer to licensed partner broker for a referral fee only where legal.
5. International: no success fees outside US until verified; info products only.
